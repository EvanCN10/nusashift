# Earth Texture Credits

Global Earth textures under `public/textures/` (`2048x1024` JPEG):

- **`day.jpg` (`2048x1024`, ~242 KB):** NASA *Blue Marble: Next Generation* with Topography and Bathymetry (July 2004 monthly composite, Reto Stöckli / NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / MODIS, public domain).
  - Catalog URL: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/blue-marble-next-generation-with-topography-and-bathymetry/
  - Direct asset: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg?w=2048
- **`bump.jpg` (`2048x1024`, ~93 KB):** NASA *Shuttle Radar Topography Mission (SRTM)* & GTOPO30 global elevation grayscale relief (`Srtm_ramp2.world.21600x10800.jpg`, public domain).
  - Source URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Srtm_ramp2.world.21600x10800.jpg
- **`specular.jpg` (`2048x1024`):** Ocean/land specular mask rasterized from public-domain Natural Earth 110m land polygons (`world-atlas/countries-110m.json`) so oceans reflect directional light subtly while landmasses and polar ice sheets remain matte.
